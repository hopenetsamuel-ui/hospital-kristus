Excellence in Africa

We bring the most advanced medical procedures to Nigeria — procedures once only available abroad are now at your doorstep.

First in Nigeria

Interventional Cardiology

Nigeria's first digital Cathlab for cardiac and neurological interventions. Angiogram, Angioplasty, Stenting & Pacing available 24/7.

First in West Africa

Robotic Knee Replacement

First in West Africa to deploy robotic knee replacement surgery using the MISSO Robotic System for precise, minimal-invasive outcomes.

Regional Pioneer

Kidney Transplant

First in South-South Nigeria to perform successful kidney transplants. Full Nephrology & Urology services with post-transplant follow-up.

Record Holder

IVF & Fertility

Comprehensive fertility diagnosis and treatment. Record-holding IVF success on a 62-year-old with triplet delivery. ICSI, IUI, Surrogacy.

Center of Excellence

Laser Surgeries

Center of excellence in laser surgeries — prostate, kidney stones, hemorrhoids, fistula and more with minimal invasive technique.

Always Available

24/7 Emergency & Trauma

Fully equipped emergency unit meeting international CPR and trauma standards. 400-pint blood bank with 24/7 ambulance services.
